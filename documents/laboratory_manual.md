## 1. Laboratory Organization
Hematology, serum chemistry, and urinalysis testing are primarily routed to the CENTRAL laboratory, with accredited local laboratory facilities utilized at specific participating investigative sites (including Site S07 and Site S08) for expedited safety monitoring.

## 2. Unit System and Conversion Standards
The Central Laboratory reports enzymatic transaminases (ALT, AST) in Conventional Units (U/L). Certain local hospital laboratories, including Site S07, calibrate analyzers using SI Katal units (µkat/L).

Conversion Standard:
- **1 µkat/L = 60.0 U/L** (for ALT and AST enzymatic activities)
- Total Bilirubin (BILI): Standard conversion is **1 mg/dL = 17.1 µmol/L**

Site-specific reference ranges listed in `reference_ranges.csv` must be applied according to the test location and analyzer calibrations.

## 3. Handling Below Quantification & Non-Numeric Values
Results marked with `<` (e.g., `<5`) denote values below the limit of quantification and must not be coerced to 0. Results marked as `ND` denote Not Detected / Not Done and indicate missing numerical data.
